ОБЪЕКТ-86 / 1986 / SIBERIAN MILITARY DISTRICT

RED CORE

An 8-minute shift in a dying Soviet reactor bunker. Keep the core alive. Someone in your crew wants it to melt. Something in the vents wants out.

1–8 PLAYERS / PHONES AND LAPTOPS / NO ACCOUNTS. BEST WITH SOUND AND FRIENDS IN THE SAME ROOM.

OBJECT-86 / STANDING ORDERS

HOW TO PLAY

ENGINEERS

Keep the core under 100% until the 8-minute lockdown. Service the reactor console, purge filters, balance coolant, seal leaks with a wrench. Fix what the saboteur breaks.

THE SABOTEUR 4+ PLAYERS

Same suit. Same mask. Your reactor services add heat instead. Reverse valves, cut breakers and camera cables. Every act takes time and leaves evidence.

SPECIMEN-09 2+ PLAYERS, OR AI SOLO

Invisible when still, a shadow when moving. Crack three mutagen safes to evolve, then pry open the surface lift. If the core melts, you die too.

NO VOTING. NO MEETINGS.

Nobody is killed by a weapon. People die in the shafts. Shove someone at a broken rail and they go over and grab the lip. If you're the one hanging, hit the beats to hold on. Below 50% you lose a hand. At 0% you fall. Anyone can hold E to pull you up. Two players holding T together tape a suspect to a pipe.
